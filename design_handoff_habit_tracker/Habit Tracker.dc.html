Habit Tracker · v1 · Aditya Chadha

Four screens, one honest record.

Dark ground first — a 4 AM habit means the app opens in the dark. Classical's paper type and hairline rules carry over: Cormorant for figures, Lora for rows, gold as stroke only. The paper sheet's navy survives as an alternative chart ramp (see Tweaks) rather than a second identity.

Tap targetThe whole row ticks. 44px minimum, 17 rows, one scroll.
ReachNavigation at the bottom; date nav sits in the header where it is read, not tapped often.
PolarityAbstain habits are marked held / broken, never completed.
RetireNo delete anywhere in the UI. Retired habits keep their history and leave the denominator.
GuardsEvery chart states its data requirement instead of drawing on thin data.
Open questions: full names for NS and NF; whether the Week screen should allow entry beyond the 7-day lock (spec says no — it is enforced here); and whether charts keep the gold ramp or the navy one.
